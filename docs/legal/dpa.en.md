# Data Processing Terms

These terms apply when you use EasyConvert to process personal data for which you are the controller, for example when your files or your application's requests contain personal data of your customers or employees. In that case you are the controller and the operator of EasyConvert is your processor. These terms are part of the [Terms of Service](/terms) and take effect when you use the service for that purpose. They contain the clauses that Article 28(3) of the GDPR and Article 26 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act require for a processing contract.

## 1. Subject, duration, nature and purpose

- **Subject.** The files, links and requests you send to EasyConvert, and the results.
- **Duration.** For as long as you use the service, and until the deletion in section 7 is complete.
- **Nature and purpose.** Automated conversion of files and delivery of the results to you. No other purpose.
- **Types of personal data.** Any personal data that your files contain, and the connection data listed in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- **Categories of data subjects.** The people whose data your files contain, and your users who send requests.

## 2. Your instructions

We process the personal data only on your documented instructions. Your use of the service, its settings and its API calls are those instructions. This includes transfers abroad, which are limited to those in section 5. If a law requires us to process the data otherwise, we tell you before we do so, unless that law forbids it. If we believe that an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you.

We take the technical and organisational measures in section 8 of the [Privacy Policy](/privacy): encryption in transit, conversion programs isolated from the network, hashed credentials, access limited to the operator, and deletion on a fixed schedule.
