# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). It lists what you must not do with EasyConvert and how to report abuse.

## 1. Forbidden content

Do not send EasyConvert files or links that contain, or that you convert in order to spread:

- sexual content involving minors, or any other obscene content whose distribution is illegal;
- content that defames a person with false facts, or that incites violence or discrimination against a person or a group;
- content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights, or that violates someone's privacy;
- malware, or content that damages or disrupts information systems, data or programs;
- content that trades personal data in breach of the law;
- content on making firearms or explosives, or on producing or trading illegal drugs;
- any other content that is illegal or that aims at, incites or helps a crime.

## 2. Forbidden actions

- Getting around the limits of your plan, for example with several accounts or by hiding your address.
- Sending requests at a rate that disrupts the service for others, or attacking the service.
- Using the URL import or the page capture to reach networks or systems that you are not allowed to reach.
- Sharing your API keys with people outside your organisation, or using someone else's key.
- Using the service for anything other than converting and processing files, for example for mining or for running other programs.

## 3. Reporting abuse

To report a file, a conversion or an account that breaks this policy, send a report through the [contact page](/contact) and include:

- what happened and when;
- the link, the job ID or the file name, if you have one;
- for a report of infringement of your rights, who you are, which right is infringed and how;
- how we can reach you.

The operator of EasyConvert receives every report and is also the designated recipient of copyright notices under the Korean Copyright Act.

## 4. What we do

We review each report without delay. When content or use breaks this policy, we stop the processing, delete the files, and suspend or close the account or the API key. For a copyright notice, we stop the reproduction or transmission and tell the person who sent the file, who can ask for it to resume by showing a right to it. We report to the authorities what the law requires us to report. We tell the person who reported the outcome, if they gave us a way to reach them.

Files are deleted shortly after conversion, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), so a report that comes later may find no file left.
